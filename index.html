Meet the game behind the Lead Under Pressure program

A research-backed approach, grounded in positive psychology

Purpose and outcome: trains strength-based thinking through cognitive reframing. The game builds self-efficacy, reduces shame around weaknesses, and creates psychological safety through humour and play. It improves active listening, communication, and empathy among leaders, and builds teamwork among team players.

The science behind the game

  • PERMA (Seligman)
  • Psychological safety (Edmondson)
  • Self-compassion (Neff)
  • Self-efficacy (Bandura)
  • Active listening (Rogers & Farson)
  • Emotional intelligence (Goleman)

Case studies

Case study 1: Psyon Games

In this pilot session, Psyon Games ran a team engagement session with Nordaurai. With the Self-Compassion game, participants found real value in opening up to each other. With the Guess the Emotions game, the team identified who tends to feel a certain way under certain circumstances, insight that will help them navigate future situations as a team.

Case study 2: Nordic Game

Participants came from different organizations and were meeting for the first time. Despite this, Nordaurai's games helped establish psychological safety quickly, and participants were open and fully engaged throughout the sessions. This showed that even among strangers, a well-designed gamified format can create trust and openness faster than traditional icebreakers or lectures. “Very good tools to start conversations and self reflection, especially in a group”.

Our story

Nordaurai was founded with the deep passion and vision of our founder, Angela Boyajian Adjounian, who spent years in the corporate world and saw how mental wellbeing and leadership trainings are passive and fail to reach everyone. Seeing the pressure in leadership, and her own mistakes due to pressure, made her talk with leaders, and it turned out that leading under pressure is a universal pain among leaders. That is why the science- and play-based Practice Leading Under Pressure program was developed as the flagship program of Nordaurai.

Angela's passion for occupational wellbeing and learning and development took her to the University of Helsinki, where she conducted research on occupational wellbeing and validated her approach with businesses and organizations in Finland and the UAE.

The answer of her validation and study at the University of Helsinki became our first flagship program: the positive psychology, play-based Practice Leading Under Pressure program.

Nordaurai goes beyond gamification in corporate learning and development. It's a real game within a program grounded in positive psychology, where pressure is simulated through games, leaders engage from the first moment, and they practice behavior change and learning.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nordaurai, and who is it for?

Nordaurai is a Finnish company for science- and play-based learning and development. Our flagship program, Practice Leading Under Pressure, helps mid-level and senior leaders practice leading under pressure through games grounded in positive psychology, with improved organizational performance and mental wellbeing at the root.

What does the Practice Leading Under Pressure program include?

The program is a self-paced e-learning program with modules and educational, science-based games as tools to practice leading under pressure. It includes our original digital games: the Delegation Game and Basketball for Compassion. Available from January 2027.

What are the outcomes of the program?

Sustainable work under pressure, improved emotional intelligence, structured decision making and execution, including delegation and seeing your team's strengths, and better communication for better team engagement, overall wellbeing, and organizational performance.

Does the program also have a physical format?

Yes. The program has a digital model and a physical model with a board game that supports leaders to practice leadership, strengths, and communication under pressure.

Is Nordaurai's play-based program backed by research or science?

Yes. The games are grounded in positive psychology (PERMA, psychological safety, self-compassion, self-efficacy and emotional intelligence), designed by a psychologist and grounded in research validation from the University of Helsinki.

What do we get with the science- and play-based Practice Leading Under Pressure program?

A structured program with original positive psychology games designed especially for the real pains of pressure. It measures your behavior and supports you to practice leading under pressure, with a test before and after the program to measure your improvement. At the end, the measurement is provided to your learning and development department.

Can we license the program and the game as a partner?

Unfortunately not. However, for any partnership matters get in touch with angela@nordaurai.com
